Important implementation choices
- f_AB is a dimensionless activation-state fraction.
- H_T = R_T/rho_RC is fixed in Fig. 3; no cumulative output-pool depletion.
- cAMP and PKAc use half-cell finite-volume boundary corrections.
- Proximal boundary is reflecting whenever source_flux is off.
- Harmonic averaging is used for D_relay at cAMP interfaces.
- PDE* is local and nondiffusing.
- k_PDE_i units are uM^-1 s^-1 because PDE* is represented in uM.
- Peak/integrated P2/P1 use equal 5-s response windows: 0-5 s and 5-10 s.
