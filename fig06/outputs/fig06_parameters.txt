FIGURE 6 REVISED PARAMETERS AND DEFINITIONS
==========================================

Shared cAMP-binding thresholds
K_B = 0.1 uM
K_A = 1 uM
K_D,eff = sqrt(K_A*K_B) = 0.316228 uM
c_max = sqrt(K_A*K_B) = 0.316228 uM

Panel A: organized RI / structured buffered diffusion
rho_R = R_T / R_* (dimensionless)
R_* is an illustrative reference abundance; no absolute RI concentration optimum is asserted.
g_R(rho_R) = 4*rho_R/(1+rho_R)^2
D_relay(c,rho_R) proportional to g_R(rho_R) * [c/(K_B+c)] * [K_A/(K_A+c)]
Displayed surface = D_relay normalized to its own maximum.
Surface maximum: rho_R = 1, c = sqrt(K_A*K_B).
rho_R plotted from 0.01 to 100.

Panel B: unorganized RI / conventional buffered diffusion
K_D,eff = sqrt(K_A*K_B)
beta = B_T / K_D,eff (dimensionless buffer abundance)
u = c / K_D,eff
kappa = beta/(1+u)^2
D_buffer/D_free = 1/(1+kappa)
beta plotted from 0.01 to 100.

Interpretive constraints
- Panel A cAMP dependence is derived from the source/acceptor occupancy closure.
- Panel A RI-abundance dependence is explicitly phenomenological and illustrative.
- R_* and g_R are not inferred from the Fig. 5 R_T optimum.
- Panel B is the conventional rapid-equilibrium buffering limit.
- The panels are limiting effective transport regimes, not parallel diffusion coefficients to be added pointwise.
- Separate colorbars are used because normalized D_relay and D_buffer/D_free are different quantities.
